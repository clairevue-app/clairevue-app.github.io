Privacy policy

Last updated : 27 septembre 2026

PousseDedans ("the app") is a houseplant care app published by Clairevue. This page describes what data is processed when you use the app.

1. No personal data collected by Clairevue

PousseDedans runs entirely offline. Everything you enter — homes, rooms, plants, care history, notes, photos — is stored only on your device, in a local database. It is never sent to a server, to Clairevue, or to any third party.

There is no user account, no sign-up, no authentication.

2. Export and backup

The app lets you export your data (homes, plants, photos, settings) to a file that you choose to save or share yourself. Clairevue has no access to those files; what happens to them depends entirely on your own choices once the file is created.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and technical data (device type, approximate IP address) in order to show ads, possibly personalised according to your Android settings.

This data is processed by Google. For more information: Google's privacy policy and ad settings.

Purchasing the full version removes these ads permanently.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The one-off purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Clairevue neither receives nor stores any payment information.

5. Notifications

Watering and fertilising reminders are local notifications, scheduled by your device from data already stored locally. No data is sent to a server to generate them.

6. Permissions requested

  • Notifications: to show care reminders (can be denied, the app works without).
  • Alarms and reminders: so the reminder fires at the time you chose.
  • Camera / gallery: only if you add a photo to a plant. Photos stay on your device.

7. Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children and knowingly collects no data about them.

8. Your rights

Since no data is stored by Clairevue, there is nothing to ask us to delete: removal happens by uninstalling the app, or by deleting a home or a photo within the app.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top of the page shows the last revision.

10. Contact

For any question: clairevue.messagerie@gmail.com
